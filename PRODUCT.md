# Thunder Accounting

Thunder Accounting is a lightweight desktop bookkeeping product for people who want to record daily income and spending with minimal friction.

## Auth surface

- Audience: returning users and new users setting up a personal ledger.
- Primary tasks: sign in, create an account, recover access, and switch between password and verification-code flows.
- Product constraints: preserve existing CloudBase/Electron authentication behavior, bilingual copy, and keyboard-operable forms.
- Visual register: the third-round login demo is the sole product visual baseline; paper/ink/gold is the default light theme, charcoal/night/gold is the switchable dark theme, with restrained motion.
- Scope: the same visual language applies to authenticated pages, personal center, settings, dialogs, tables, forms, and status labels; existing business behavior and authentication semantics remain unchanged.
